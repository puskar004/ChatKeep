ChatKeep

Private chat backup

Keep a copy before you delete

  1. Open WhatsApp on this phone.
  2. Open the chat you want to keep.
  3. Tap the contact or group name (iPhone) or the ⋮ menu (Android).
  4. Choose Export chat. Use Without media for a faster file.
  5. Share or Save the file, then tap Choose export file below.
  6. You can delete the chat in WhatsApp. ChatKeep keeps your copy on this phone.
